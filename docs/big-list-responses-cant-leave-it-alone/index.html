A big list of responses to “You can leave the Church, but you can't leave it alone”

resource apologetics-responses compilation Updated Nov 18, 2026

The list below is a satirical response to the refrain “You can leave the Church, but you can’t leave it alone.” It compares the attention former members give the LDS Church with the attention the Church and its members give to those who do not belong. It is reproduced from a 2015 post on the exmormon subreddit (archived copy) by u/Gileriodekel, who adapted an earlier post by u/usernamerevoked, posted with permission from u/PostMormon. The text remains theirs.

The analysis that refers to this list is You can leave the Church but you can’t leave it alone.

The list

  • “Every Exmormon a missionary” is not a thing.
  • Exmormons don’t go knocking on their neighbor’s doors to tell them about Exmormonism.
  • Exmormons don’t text friends saying, “I noticed you went to Church today. I sure missed you at not-Church. Hope to see you soon.”
  • Exmormons don’t bribe their neighbor’s kids with treats to stay home from Church.
  • When PBS has a show on about Richard Dawkins, Exmormons don’t make fancy invitations and invite all their Mormon friends over to watch it.
  • When their Mormon neighbors move, Exmormons don’t track them down and invite them to leave the Church in their new area.
  • Exmormons don’t fly across the country/world their own expense and then spend two years trying to get Mormons to leave the Church.
  • Exmormons occasionally quote Carl Sagan and Neil de Grasse Tyson, but they don’t flood Facebook with such quotes the week before and after the Nobel Prize for Science is presented.
  • Exmormons don’t put on huge Exmormon pageants every year across the country and invite all of their Mormon friends to attend.
  • Exmormons don’t secretly interrogate their neighbor’s children to find out why their parents are staying in Church.
  • Exmormons don’t drive down to the Church on the night they’re having youth activities and say, “Hey kids, we’re on our way to the arcade. Do you want a ride?”
  • Exmormons don’t chastise Mormon family members for posting something on Facebook that was too “Mormony”.
  • When a Mormon neighbor makes the decision to stay in the Church, Exmormons don’t write them a letter explaining the eternal consequences of their decision and telling them that before they make such an important decision they must sit down with them to discuss it.
  • Exmormons don’t give their Mormon friends books by Jerald & Sandra Tanner for Christmas and Birthday presents.
  • Exmormons don’t send their teenage sons to their Mormon neighbor’s house to collect donations for Exmormons that have fallen on hard times.
  • Exmormons don’t email Brother Jake videos to their Mormon friends.
  • Exmormons never look at a Mormon and judge them for what type of underwear they’re wearing.
  • Exmormons never use funerals as an opportunity to teach the plan of natural selection to grieving Mormons.
  • Exmormons never write their testimony inside a copy of “No Man Knows My History” and give it to a Mormon relative while explaining what the book has meant to them and how it has changed their life.
  • Exmormons don’t turn on a John Dehlin Mormon Stories podcast to listen to when they’re giving their Mormon friend a ride home because their car broke down.
  • Exmormons don’t purchase a gift subscription to a Secular Humanist Magazine for their neighbors.
  • Exmormons don’t end all messages, texts, and emails to their Mormon family and friends by stating, “The Gospel is not true!”
  • When you are sick or injured I don’t ask if you want a “negation of priesthood” blessing to remove the harm that magical thinking does when you are trying to get better. When you clearly express that you are not interested I don’t keep bugging you about it, saying, “Are you sure? Because my sister’s kid had a tumor and he had a negation of priesthood blessing done and the doctors looked again and they said his tumor was gone, just like that!”
  • Schools located in areas dominated by Exmormons never send kids home from school for being dressed too “mormony”.
  • Schools located in areas dominated by Exmormons never have “CES Letter” days.
  • Exmormons do not send Birthday cards to their Mormon neighbor’s children with a note that says, “Jeremy Runnells loves you and so do I. We sure miss you when you at Not-Church.”
  • Exmormons don’t tell their Mormon friends that they’re going to hell because they don’t believe in the Big Bang.
  • Exmormons don’t invite their Mormon friends to dinner and surprise them by having also invited two young evolutionary biologists who would like to share a message with them.
  • Exmormons don’t assume that their Mormon friends are just going through a temporary Mormon phase because they were offended by a Exmormon, were accidentally exposed to some Pro-Mormon literature, or had the desire to feel excessive guilt for their sins.
  • When Exmormons notice a family in their neighborhood has attended Church for several weeks in a row, they don’t leave delicious chocolate cupcakes on their doorstep with a note that says they wish you would stop attending Church so often.
  • Exmormons don’t call a meeting with other Exmormons and say, “Hey, lets make a list of all the Mormons in the ward boundaries, and let’s think about it and pray about it, and we’ll narrow that list down to 5 names, and we’ll focus all of our energy and attention on these five people or families, and we’ll try to get them to leave the Church”.
  • Exmormons don’t deny Mormon friends and family the opportunity to attend weddings unless they’re “an Exmormon in good standing”.
  • Exmormons don’t hold posthumous church resignation ceremonies for their Mormon loved ones who have passed away.
  • Exmormons don’t disown their children if they decide to be Mormon.
  • Exmormons don’t counsel anyone to divorce their spouse because they’ve decided that Atheism isn’t true and that Mormonism makes more sense.
  • Exmormons don’t tell Mormons that they are members of a cult, and are deceived by men.
  • No Mormon has ever begun using drugs or alcohol to mask the pain caused by their Exmormon friends who couldn’t accept their belief in The Book of Mormon.
  • No Mormon has ever committed suicide because their Exmormon friends, family, and community rejected them because they wanted to marry another Mormon.
  • Exmormons don’t contribute funds nor canvas neighborhoods in support of legislation prohibiting temple marriages as “non-traditional.”
